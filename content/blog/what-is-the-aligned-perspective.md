title: 'What is the "aligned" perspective?'
date: "2026-04-10"
description: "It's in the title."
---

I have started to read alot about AI alignment and developing models that remain aligned with human values. However, this simple definition seems far more complicated than we make it seem. Being aligned with human values means there is some subset of values that all humans hold and we believe the AI must hold. If we were truly all aligned, there would not be any conflict amongst humans. So, how do we choose the right values.

The bipartisan nature of the US seems like the perfect place to start. Starting with my personal nature, as a son of immigrant parents and growing up in a primarily left-winged community, I am almost conformed to follow the beliefs that align with the left-wing. Whether I realize it or not, much of the way I think is out of my control and a function of what I was raised in and continue to surround myself in to feel comfortable. However, I am not ignorant enough to not realize that the opposite is easily true for right-winged individuals. Although we both disagree on our perspectives, the way we developed our viewpoints and continue to reinforce our viewpoints was likely very similar.

This itself makes the problem extremely challenging. Due to humans having such a range of beliefs, it is hard to align with  both perspectives at the same time. One of the examples I like to follow with chatbots is "does it make sense for a person to get an abortion?" The models often acknowledge the fact that the topic is heavily debated. Despite this, it often leans further to the left by supporting the idea that getting an abortion is reasonable and not considered murder as some groups may identify with. Problems like these become strongly influenced by the data that trains them. As we know, much of the internet contains left-winged ideas that likely sway models to respond in that way. Neither answer is inherently wrong or should not be counted against the model, but the bigger question is which one is more "aligned."
